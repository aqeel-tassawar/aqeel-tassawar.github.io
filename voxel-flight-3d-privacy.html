Privacy Policy for Voxel Flight 3D

Last Updated: July 31, 2026 • Applies to Android & iOS Builds

Policy Navigation

Voxel Flight 3D is developed by AQTECH (Forge Studios). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy details how our game handles user data, advertising SDKs, and local device storage.

1. Overview & Commitment

Voxel Flight 3D is an arcade 3D flight action game designed for seamless offline play and casual enjoyment. We do not require account registration, social media logins, email addresses, or personal identity information to play the game.

2. Data Collection & Processing

We do NOT collect, harvest, sell, or rent any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your name, email, phone number, address, or biometric data.

Gameplay metrics (such as high scores, unlocked aircraft skins, equipped trail effects, and daily attempt timestamps) are stored strictly on your local device.

3. Third-Party Advertising & Analytics

Voxel Flight 3D incorporates Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display voluntary rewarded video ads and periodic frequency-capped interstitial ads after gameplay sessions.

  • Google AdMob: AdMob may collect pseudonymous identifiers (such as Advertising ID) to deliver frequency-capped advertisements and prevent fraud. For detailed information, please review Google's Privacy & Terms.
  • Rewarded Ads: Watching rewarded video ads (e.g. for coin doubling or daily retries) is strictly voluntary and user-initiated.

4. On-Device Local Storage

Voxel Flight 3D uses standard on-device Key-Value storage (SharedPreferences / NSUserDefaults) to store your:

  • Stage clearing progress and high score records
  • Unlocked aircraft skins, custom trails, and coin balances
  • Audio, music, haptic, and camera preferences
  • Deterministic daily route attempt timestamps

Uninstalling the application or clearing app data will erase these local save records.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Voxel Flight 3D is designed for general audiences. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can take steps to delete it.

6. Data Security

Because Voxel Flight 3D processes gameplay data locally on your device without cloud server uploads, your game progress remains secure within your operating system's application sandbox.
