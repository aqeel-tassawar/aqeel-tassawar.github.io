SlimShot — Privacy Policy

ForgeLabs  ·  com.forgelabs.slimshot

Your privacy matters. SlimShot is built on a simple principle: your photos never leave your device. All photo analysis and cleanup happens entirely on-device, with no uploads, no accounts, and no cloud storage.

1. What We Collect

SlimShot requests only the permissions it needs to perform on-device photo analysis. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers.

  • Photo & media access — required to scan your gallery for blurry, duplicate, dark, screenshot, and burst photos. All scanning happens locally on your device.
  • Storage access — required to delete photos you explicitly choose to remove. No file is deleted without your direct confirmation.
On-Device Only: Photos, thumbnails, and analysis results are processed entirely on your device. No image data, metadata, or analysis results are ever transmitted to ForgeLabs or any third party.

We do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, or any account information
  • Location data
  • Contacts or call logs
  • Crash reports or analytics events

No account, login, or registration is required to use SlimShot.

2. How We Use Information

The only processing that takes place is on your device, solely to provide the app's core features:

  • Scanning your gallery to detect blurry, dark, duplicate, screenshot, and burst photos
  • Displaying categorised results so you can review them before taking any action
  • Permanently deleting only the photos you explicitly select and confirm for deletion

We do not use any information for profiling, marketing, or any purpose beyond operating the app's features described above.

3. Google AdMob Third-Party

SlimShot displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob (banner and interstitial formats). AdMob is operated by Google LLC and is subject to Google's own privacy practices.

To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect and use:

  • Advertising identifiers (Android Advertising ID / GAID)
  • Device information (model, OS version, screen size)
  • General network information (IP address, connection type)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

We have configured AdMob with the following settings to protect users:

  • Maximum ad content rating: PG (Parental Guidance)
  • tagForUnderAgeOfConsent is enabled, directing AdMob to treat all ad requests as coming from users who may be under the age of consent and to limit ad personalisation accordingly.

For full details on how Google uses data for advertising purposes, please review Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob's data practices:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data when you use our partners' apps

You can reset or opt out of interest-based advertising at any time through your device's advertising settings: Settings → Google → Ads.

4. Data Storage & Security

Because all processing is performed on-device, there is no external database or server storing your photo data or personal information. App preferences (such as filter settings) are stored only in your device's local app storage and are removed when you uninstall the app.

Deleted photos are moved to the system trash or permanently removed, depending on your Android version and device settings. We recommend reviewing the Android Recycle Bin / Trash if you wish to recover a deleted photo shortly after removal.

We do not maintain or have access to any copy of your photos at any point.

5. Children's Privacy

SlimShot is a general-audience app. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction).

As noted above, we have enabled AdMob's tagForUnderAgeOfConsent flag to apply additional protections to ad serving for all users. No ad network data collection beyond what is described in Section 3 is permitted through our configuration.

If you believe a child has provided personal data through the app in a way not described in this policy, please contact us at the address below and we will investigate promptly.

6. Your Rights

Because SlimShot does not collect or store personal data on external servers, most traditional data-subject rights (access, portability, erasure) are satisfied automatically — there is simply no data held by us to access, correct, or delete.

You may, however:

  • Revoke permissions at any time via your device's app settings (Settings → Apps → SlimShot → Permissions). Revoking photo or storage access will prevent the app from scanning or deleting photos.
  • Opt out of personalised ads by resetting your Advertising ID or enabling "Opt out of Ads Personalisation" in your device's Google settings.
  • Uninstall the app to remove all locally stored app data from your device.

If you are located in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or California, and believe you have a data-related concern, you are welcome to contact us using the details below.
